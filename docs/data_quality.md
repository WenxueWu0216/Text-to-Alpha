- Per year: 2018: 24,825 | 2019: 29,090 | 2020: 31,635 | 2021: 20,249 | 2022: 38,107 | 2023: 57,613

- META has no articles: FNSPID most likely files it under its pre-2022 symbol FB, which is not in the universe. Not verified (needs the raw CSV); fixing it means adding FB as an alias of META and rebuilding from raw.

## Timestamps
- Date-only (no time of day): **200,930 (99.7%)**; with a real time: 589
- Unparseable timestamps remaining in processed data: 0
- Handling: date-only rows are treated as published 23:59 New York time (after the close), so they trade at the NEXT open; unparseable rows are dropped in step 1.

## Duplicates
- Residual same-ticker, same-title, same-day duplicates after dedup: 0
- Titles tagged to more than one ticker: 29,640
- Handling: same ticker + normalised title within 3 days -> keep the earliest copy; the same story tagged to several tickers is kept once per ticker.

## Ticker mapping
- Articles whose headline + lead never name the tagged company: **69,783 (34.6%)**
- Handling: FNSPID's Stock_symbol is taken as given; articles that never name the company are kept (dropping them would use a hand-made filter not applied to all methods) and reported here as a known source of label noise.
